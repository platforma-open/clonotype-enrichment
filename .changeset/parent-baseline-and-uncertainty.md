Parent-normalized enrichment and measurement error for designed libraries. When the dataset carries a mutation count and a parent link, every enrichment also gets a value relative to the variant's own parent. When a nucleotide dataset links to its proteins, the block pools reads to the protein level and reports an error for each value, from read counts and from the scatter of synonymous variants.
